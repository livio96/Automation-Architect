Map one process

Describe a single business process: what starts it, what “done” looks like, who touches it, which tools are involved, and what frustrates you about it. The architect maps that one process end to end — main path, variants and exceptions — through five gated phases and ends with an interactive dashboard the people who run it can work from. Plan on 30–60 minutes, or front-load every step you know and it will switch to Fast Track.

Process: Amazon FBA customer returns. It starts when Amazon notifies us of a return and ends when the unit is restocked, sent back to the vendor, or scrapped and the refund is reconciled in NetSuite. Two warehouse people receive and inspect, one customer-service rep handles the Amazon case, and accounting reconciles the refund. Tools: Amazon Seller Central, NetSuite, a shared Excel log, Outlook. We spend about 15 hours a week on it, and about a third of returns show up with no RMA number so someone has to figure out what the item is.
  1. Stick to one process. If you have several, the architect will ask which to start with.
  2. Answer the follow-up questions with real numbers. "4 hrs/week" beats "too long."
  3. Confirm or correct each phase before the architect moves on.
  4. When the dashboard appears, open it, save it, and share the file with your team.
